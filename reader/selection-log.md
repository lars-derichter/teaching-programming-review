# Selection log

How the fifteen articles in this reader were chosen (July 2026).

## Process

1. **Candidate list.** From the literature review in this repository, 21
   candidate articles were identified across its themes, deliberately mixing
   perspectives: cognitive/experimental, large-scale statistical,
   theoretical/mechanistic, qualitative, systematic reviews, practitioner
   syntheses, and the generative AI literature.
2. **Acquisition.** All 21 full texts were downloaded: open access papers
   directly (PLOS, Springer); ACM Digital Library papers via its open access
   programme; Taylor & Francis papers through the Open Universiteit library
   (EZproxy, logged-in Chrome session). All PDFs verified as complete
   full-text versions.
3. **Reading.** Every candidate was read in full (for the two very long
   working-group/review reports, all core sections plus conclusions) before
   the final keep-or-replace decision, as required by the assignment.
4. **Selection.** Trimmed to 15 to respect the 10–15 budget, optimising for:
   coverage of the review's themes, diversity of perspective and method,
   direct usefulness to future graduaat lecturers, and readability supported
   by the introductions.

## Decisions

Kept (15): Brown & Wilson 2018; Lister et al. 2004; Sorva 2013; Patitsas et
al. 2020; Robins 2010; Kirschner et al. 2006; Xie et al. 2019; Sentance et
al. 2019; Margulieux et al. 2020; Keuning et al. 2018; Kinnunen & Simon 2012;
Vihavainen et al. 2011; Lyon & Denner 2019; Denny et al. 2024; Prather et al.
2024.

Set aside after full reading (6, in `articles/extra/` with reasons in its
README): Porter et al. 2013 (covered via Brown & Wilson tip 2 and theme 5
intro — the hardest cut); Ben-Ari 1998 (argument covered in Sorva §5; PDF is
a scan); Qian & Lehman 2017 (overlaps Sorva/Lister); Ericson et al. 2022
(too long; headline in theme 5 intro); Vihavainen et al. 2014 (headline
figure cited in intro and theme 3); Quille & Bergin 2019 (summarised in
theme 7 intro; Kinnunen & Simon covers the affect theme more formatively).

Notable switches during reading: Kinnunen & Simon was chosen over Quille &
Bergin for the affect slot after comparing both in full (the emic,
qualitative perspective was judged more formative for teacher-students and
adds methodological breadth); Lyon & Denner was kept over Vihavainen et al.
2014 for the second vocational slot because the population perspective is
the distinguishing feature of the target course.
